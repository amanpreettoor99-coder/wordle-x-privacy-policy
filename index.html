Wordle X Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Wordle X is an original word puzzle game developed as an independent project. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Wordle X mobile application.

Information Stored by the App

Wordle X may store information locally on your device to provide game features. This may include:

  • Game progress and saved games
  • Game statistics and history
  • Streaks and daily challenge progress
  • Achievements, badges, XP and level information
  • Your chosen username
  • Game settings and preferences

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the features of the game.

Information We Collect

Wordle X does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address in order to play the game.

Wordle X does not currently use advertising services, third-party analytics, or user accounts.

Internet and Data Sharing

Wordle X does not currently send your game progress, statistics, username, or settings to a developer-operated server or cloud account.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Children's Privacy

Wordle X is a general-audience word puzzle game. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Information stored locally by the app remains on your device and is subject to the security protections provided by your device and operating system.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the features or data practices of Wordle X change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Wordle X, please contact us at wordlex.game@gmail.com
