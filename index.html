Wordle X Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Wordle X is an original word puzzle game developed as an independent project. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Wordle X mobile application.

Information Stored by the App

Wordle X may store information locally on your device to provide game features. This may include:

  • Game progress and saved games
  • Game statistics and history
  • Streaks and Daily Challenge progress
  • Achievements, badges, XP and level information
  • Your chosen username
  • Game settings and preferences

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is used to provide the features of the game. Wordle X does not require you to create an account.

Analytics

Wordle X uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve the game.

Analytics may collect information such as:

  • App sessions and session duration
  • App opens and general app usage
  • Operating system and device information
  • An app-instance identifier used for analytics
  • Coarse geographic information derived by the analytics service
  • Gameplay and feature-usage events

Wordle X also sends custom analytics events that may describe actions such as starting or completing a game, winning or losing, the selected word-length mode, Daily Challenge activity, and use of features such as Statistics, Profile, Achievements and Settings.

Wordle X does not intentionally send your username, email address, phone number, physical address, or other directly identifying personal information to Google Analytics through its custom analytics events.

Analytics information is used for purposes such as understanding usage, measuring engagement, improving gameplay and features, and identifying areas where the app can be improved.

Google Analytics for Firebase is provided by Google. Analytics data is handled according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

For more information about Google's handling of data through Firebase Analytics, please see Google's Firebase Privacy and Security documentation .

Information We Do Not Require

Wordle X does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address in order to play the game.

Wordle X does not use advertising services in the current version of the app and does not use analytics for personalised advertising.

Internet and Data Sharing

Wordle X does not operate its own user account or game-progress cloud service. Your saved game information, statistics, username and settings are intended to remain stored locally on your device.

Analytics information is transmitted to Google through Google Analytics for Firebase so that app usage can be measured and analysed.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not intentionally share your username, email address, or other directly identifying personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

Wordle X uses third-party services to provide certain technical functionality.

The current version uses:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase for app analytics and usage measurement.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

Wordle X is a general-audience word puzzle game.

Wordle X does not knowingly request or require children to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The app does not provide user-to-user messaging or social communication features.

Data Security

Information stored locally by the app remains on your device and is subject to the security protections provided by your device and operating system.

Analytics information transmitted to Google Analytics for Firebase is protected in transit using encryption provided by the service.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored Wordle X data remains on your device until it is deleted or cleared by you or by the operating system.

Because Wordle X does not require a user account, there is no Wordle X account that you need to delete.

Analytics data is retained and managed by Google Analytics according to the configuration of the analytics service and Google's applicable policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the features or data practices of Wordle X change.

Any updated version will be published on this page, together with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Wordle X, please contact:

Email: wordlex.game@gmail.com
